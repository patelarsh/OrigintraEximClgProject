Frequently Asked Questions About Importing Spices from India

Who is the leading spices exporter in India for international bulk buyers?

Origintra Exim is a trusted merchant spices exporter in India, registered with the Spices Board of India, APEDA, and FSSAI. We supply containerized consignments (FCL/LCL) of premium whole spices, sortex seeds, and finely milled powders to commercial importers in over 24 countries worldwide.

What spices does Origintra Exim export from India?

We export 27+ commodities across Whole Spices (Dried Red Chilli, Green Cardamom, Malabar Black Pepper, Cinnamon, Clove), Ground Spices (Chilli Powder, Turmeric, Coriander, Cumin), Seeds (Sortex Cumin, Coriander, Mustard, Fenugreek, Fennel), and Authentic Masala Blends.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for bulk spice exports?

Our standard commercial MOQ is 1 x 20ft Full Container Load (FCL) (typically 12 to 19 Metric Tons). For new buyers or trial lots, consolidated multi-commodity mixed containers and commercial evaluation samples are available.

Which Indian sea ports do you dispatch spice export containers from?

We ship primarily from Mundra Port (Gujarat) and JNPT / Nhava Sheva (Maharashtra) for seed spices and chillies, and Cochin Port (Kerala) and Tuticorin for southern hill spices. Shipping terms include FOB, CFR, and CIF.
